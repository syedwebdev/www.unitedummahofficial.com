04

Daily Progress

HOW THE PROGRAM WORKS.

The Deen Foundation Program is designed for Muslims living in today’s world.

A world of distractions.

A world of weak attention spans.

A world where consistency is difficult.

A world where many people want change but cannot even stay committed to one daily task.

That is why the program is built around simple but serious daily progress.

20-30

Members are expected to give a minimum of twenty to thirty minutes daily.

They complete the assigned learning task.

They submit a summary.

The summary can be written or sent as a voice note.

Coordinators track progress and help members stay accountable.

The goal is not to burden people.

The goal is to make them consistent.

Small daily effort becomes discipline.

Discipline becomes transformation.

Transformation becomes preparation.
05

Active Learning

SUMMARY SUBMISSION SYSTEM.

Members do not only watch content and disappear.

They submit summaries so the learning becomes active.

This helps members reflect, remember, and explain what they learned.

It also helps coordinators track seriousness and consistency.

Summary format:

Full Name:

Course or Task Name:

Coordinator:

Summary or Voice Note:

This system is simple.

But it separates serious members from temporary excitement.

Anyone can feel emotional for one night.

But preparation requires consistency.

Local Revival

MASJID AND MADRASSAH REVIVAL.

LOCAL

Members who move towards Masjid And Madrassah Revival work to support the revival of Islamic learning at the local level.

This front helps strengthen Masjids and Madrassahs with Quran understanding, Deen foundation, Seerah, Akhirah, Islamic systems, and practical Islamic learning alongside existing religious education.

This work is done with respect.

Not with arrogance.

Not with conflict.

Not with disrespect towards Imams or committees.

A member who enters this front must have adab, patience, clarity, and discipline.
