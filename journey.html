THE ORIGIN OF UNITED UMMAH

United Ummah was founded by Abdullah Kashif after this breaking point, when temporary emotion and online reaction were no longer acceptable.

THE REALIZATION

FROM REACTION
TO STRATEGY

At first, many people were saying the same thing. Open the borders. We will go ourselves.

But research exposed a harder reality. Palestine was not facing an unorganized street conflict. It was facing a military structure backed by weapons, aircraft, state power, and international support.

Untrained civilians moving emotionally could not create the strategic result needed on the ground.

Real intervention required state level power. It required Parliament. It required legal direction. It required national pressure.

THE FIRST CAMPAIGN

THE PALESTINE
DEFENCE BILL

That is where the idea of the Palestine Defence Bill was born.

The demand was clear. The Parliament of Pakistan should pass a bill giving the Pakistan Army legal and political approval for strategic intervention in support of Palestine.

The aim was not another slogan, another emotional speech, or another temporary protest.

THE AIM

Build a demand capable of reaching Parliament, gathering public pressure, and pushing the state towards responsibility.

THE FIRST SERIOUS STEP

ISTIFTA
& FATAWA

The next step was not shouting. It was an istifta.

An istifta is a formal written question submitted to qualified muftis. When the muftis answer that question, their response becomes a fatwa.

United Ummah prepared and submitted istifta documents asking about the responsibility of the Parliament of Pakistan, public pressure through legal means, and political accountability around the Palestine Defence Bill.

FATWA 01

Darul Ifta, Jamia Sayyiduna Abu Dharr al Ghifari

FATWA 02

Darul Ifta, Muadh bin Jabal, Karachi

After serious effort, Alhamdulillah, two fatawas were received, giving the campaign a stronger religious foundation.

View Fatawas View Istifta Documents
BUILDING NATIONAL PRESSURE

FROM RELIGIOUS BACKING
TO PUBLIC PRESSURE

After receiving the fatawas, United Ummah started preparing for the next stage.

The plan was to take the demand to major scholars and muftis, seek endorsements, build wider religious support, and push the issue towards national attention.

This was not meant to remain limited to one person, one page, or one circle.

It had to become a serious national demand.

United Ummah prepared endorsement proposals, online campaign plans, physical campaign plans, and pressure building strategies for the Palestine Defence Bill campaign.

THE PUBLIC PETITION
7,100+

To build public pressure, United Ummah launched an online petition for the Palestine Defence Bill.

The purpose was to show that this demand was not coming from one individual alone. Muslims wanted Pakistan to move beyond statements. Muslims wanted a serious response from the Parliament of Pakistan. Muslims wanted action for Palestine.

Alhamdulillah, the petition received more than 7100 signatures.

WHEN THE MISSION EXPANDED

GLOBAL SUMUD
FLOTILLA

During this period, the Global Sumud Flotilla mission began.

Activists from around the world sailed towards Gaza to challenge the illegal blockade.

The Pakistani delegation was led by former Senator Mushtaq Ahmad Khan. Israeli forces detained the activists, and the mission became a major international issue.

UNITED UMMAH'S ROLE

United Ummah joined Palestine Action Coalition and supported the campaign through online awareness, fundraising support, physical protest support, and the release campaign for former Senator Mushtaq Ahmad Khan.

This stage showed that digital pressure has value when it is organized with purpose.

It also showed something deeper.

The Ummah needs voices, but voices alone are not enough.

The Ummah needs organization, discipline, networks, and strength behind those voices.

THE TURNING POINT

THE LIMIT
OF PRESSURE

After former Senator Mushtaq Ahmad Khan was released and returned to Pakistan, United Ummah returned to the Palestine Defence Bill endorsement campaign.

The demand was still alive.

The mission was still clear.

But then came a turning point.

THE CALL

United Ummah received a call from relevant authorities indicating that the demand had reached them and that the campaign should be ended.

The campaign had to be stopped.

THAT MOMENT EXPOSED A PAINFUL TRUTH

We had voice, but not enough power.

We had pressure, but not enough structure.

We had people reacting, but not enough people prepared.

We could raise an issue, but we did not yet have the deep strength needed to carry it all the way.

This became one of the most important lessons in the journey of United Ummah.

The problem was not only one campaign.

The problem was the condition of the Ummah.

FROM CAMPAIGNS TO PREPARATION FOR IMAM MEHDI

FROM CAMPAIGNS
TO PREPARATION.

After these campaigns and experiences, United Ummah fully focused on building a complete plan for Preparation for Imam Mehdi.

This plan is not built on random motivation.

It is not built on temporary online energy.

It is not built on emotional speeches.

It is a structured system to rebuild Muslims from the foundation and then move them towards practical responsibility.

United Ummah does not claim to be the only effort working for the Ummah.

It does not claim divine authority.

It is simply one structured effort to prepare Muslims through education, discipline, organization, financial strength, media work, and community revival.

The system begins with United Ummah University, the education and training wing of United Ummah.

Its first entry gate is the Deen Foundation Program.

Anyone who wants to become a serious part of United Ummah must first enter through the Deen Foundation Program, rebuild their Deen foundation, follow the roadmap, develop discipline, and prepare themselves for responsibility.
