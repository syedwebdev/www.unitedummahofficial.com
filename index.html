What United Ummah Is

A MOVEMENT BUILT FOR RESPONSIBILITY.

United Ummah is a movement built to prepare Muslims spiritually, intellectually, socially, financially, and practically for responsibility.

The mission is not to create temporary emotion.

The mission is to rebuild Muslims from the foundation and move them towards structured work.

This work is connected to Preparation for Imam Mehdi, but United Ummah does not claim divine authority and does not claim to be the only effort working for the Ummah.

United Ummah is one structured effort.

The claim is humble. The mission is serious.

Masjid And Madrassah Revival

REVIVE WITH WISDOM & RESPECT.

The Masjid was never meant to be only a place where Muslims pray and leave.

The Madrassah was never meant to produce children who can read the Quran but do not understand what Allah is saying.

Masjid And Madrassah Revival is built to strengthen Islamic learning at the local level.

This front works to support Masjids and Madrassahs with Quran understanding, Deen foundation, Seerah, Akhirah, Islamic systems, and practical Islamic learning alongside existing religious education.

The purpose is not to fight Imams.

Not to disrespect committees.

Not to attack Madrassah teachers.

The purpose is to support, strengthen, and revive with wisdom and respect.
