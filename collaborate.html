04

Local Revival

MASJID AND MADRASSAH REVIVAL COLLABORATION

If you want to collaborate on Masjid And Madrassah Revival, your proposal should help strengthen Islamic learning at the local level.

This can include support for Masjids, Madrassahs, local teams, curriculum execution, appeals, petitions, parents, students, Imams, committees, society offices, or community based learning.

This front is not about fighting Masjids.

It is not about disrespecting Imams.

It is not about creating conflict with committees.

It is about strengthening existing religious spaces with wisdom, respect, and structure.

A collaboration in this front can help introduce Quran understanding, Deen foundation, Seerah, Akhirah, Islamic systems, and practical Islamic learning alongside existing Madrassah education.

If you are connected to a Masjid, Madrassah, society, local community, education setup, Islamic learning circle, parents group, or local team, you can propose a collaboration for this front.

Your proposal should clearly explain what access, support, space, students, resources, or local execution you can provide.

If you want to understand this front first, continue to the Masjid And Madrassah Revival page.

Strength For Responsibility

UNITED UMMAH NETWORK COLLABORATION

07

The Proposal Blueprint

WHAT A PROPOSAL SHOULD INCLUDE

If you want to collaborate with United Ummah, do not send a vague message.

Send a clear proposal.

  1. Your proposal should include your name or organization name.
  2. Your city and country.
  3. The front you want to collaborate on.
  4. What work you want to do.
  5. What experience, access, skill, platform, team, or resource you already have.
  6. How your collaboration can help United Ummah.
  7. What support you need from United Ummah.
  8. Your contact details.
  9. Any relevant links, profiles, documents, previous work, or proof of execution.

A serious proposal makes the work easier to understand.

A clear proposal shows that you respect the mission.

A vague proposal delays the work.
