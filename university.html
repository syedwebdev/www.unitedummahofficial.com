What This University Is

NOT A NORMAL UNIVERSITY.

United Ummah University is not a normal university.

It is not built for degrees.

It is not built for certificates.

It is not built to create people who only collect information and then remain the same.

United Ummah University is the education and training system of United Ummah.

It exists to rebuild Muslims spiritually, intellectually, socially, and practically for Preparation for Imam Mehdi.

The Ummah cannot be rebuilt by emotion alone.

It cannot be rebuilt by posts alone.

It cannot be rebuilt by slogans alone.

It cannot be rebuilt by people who want responsibility but have never been trained for it.

This is why United Ummah University exists.

To rebuild the Muslim before calling him towards the work of the Ummah.

To give him foundation before responsibility.

To give him direction before action.

To give him discipline before leadership.

To prepare him before he is asked to rise.
